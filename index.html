Frequently Asked Questions

What is Damascus steel?

Modern pattern-welded Damascus steel is made by forge-welding layers of different steels to create distinctive patterns.

What makes a handmade knife different?

Its design, materials, construction and finishing reflect the maker's choices and craftsmanship.

What is the history of Viking axes?

Axes were important tools in Viking Age Scandinavian society, with different forms used for everyday work and other historical purposes.
